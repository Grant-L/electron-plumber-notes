# The Electron Plumber — episode notes

Companion notes for the [The Electron Plumber](https://www.youtube.com/@TheElectronPlumber)
YouTube channel: learning goals, episode notes, readings, simulations, and
standing corrections for each episode.

The channel is one question, pursued honestly: **what is an electron?** We
know its behavior to extraordinary accuracy; we do not know what it *is*.
I plumb electrons for a living, I just want to know what they ARE

Every episode is labeled — historical record, established physics, explicit
speculation, or shop practice — and these notes carry the labels, the sources,
and the errata.
